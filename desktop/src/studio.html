What this demo really does

Real
  • Your files are decoded and played locally with the Web Audio API.
  • Built-in drums, bass, pad, keys and the example vocal are synthesized in code, so no licensed samples are involved.
  • Every effect runs live: pitch, reverse, chop, gate length, fit to tempo, filter, drive, delay, reverb, volume, pan.
  • Record turns your virtual keyboard playing into a pattern, and a mic take into a new sound and track, lined up with bar 1.
  • Play live turns any sound, yours included, into a playable instrument from the on-screen keys, your computer keyboard, or a MIDI keyboard where the browser allows it.
  • "Arrange it" asks Claude for a pattern built only from the sounds listed above, when this view allows it. Otherwise a keyword matcher does it.
  • AI producer settings shape every run: personality, creativity, work mode (suggest, preview or auto), explained changes, takes, reference vibe, project memory, Keep my sound, protected tracks, built-in sound rules, lyric helper, snapshots with undo, a monthly credit budget, and a view of exactly what the AI is sent.
  • Export renders the song offline to a 16- or 24-bit WAV, at the length and sample rate set in Settings.
Faked or missing
  • No accounts, cloud library or saved projects yet. Refresh and it's gone.
  • "Fit to tempo" changes speed and pitch together. A real stretcher keeps the pitch.
  • No third-party plugins. Browsers can't load VST or AU; they need Web Audio Modules.
  • Mic recording uses whatever mic the browser allows, with no monitoring, input level or audio interface choice. The desktop app records from your interface.
  • No stem splitting, key detection or beat detection on your uploads.
  • One 16-step pattern per track. A real song needs sections and longer phrases.
