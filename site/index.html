How it works

Three steps from idea to track.

A plain-words prompt does the arranging. You keep full control of every step afterwards.

+ my_vocal.wav
01

Pick your sounds

Start from the free starter library, drop in your own WAVs, loops and vocals, or record straight from your mic.

›dark trap at 140, my guitar reversed
02

Describe the song

“Dark trap at 140, my guitar pitched down and reversed.” Loopwright picks the steps, effects and levels from your library.

03

Shape it and export

Edit any step, play parts live on the on-screen keyboard, tweak the effects, then export a WAV.

Your music stays yours

The AI arranges. It never makes the audio.

Loopwright only decides which of your sounds play when, and with which effects. Every note you hear comes from the starter library or from you.

  • Your audio stays on your computer. Only sound names and lengths go to the arranger, never the recordings.
  • Starter sounds are license-free. Use them in anything you put out.
  • Credits you can see. 1 credit is 1 cent, you see the most a run can cost, and failed runs are free.
