How it works

A plain-words prompt does the arranging. You keep full control of every step afterwards.

01

Bring your sounds

Drop in WAVs, loops, one-shots and vocals. Built-in basic drums, bass and keys are made in code, so there are no stock samples to license.

02

Describe the song

"Dark trap at 140, my sample pitched down and reversed." Loopwright picks steps, effects and levels using only what's in your library.

03

Shape it and export

Edit the steps, play parts live on the on-screen keyboard, tweak effects, then export a WAV.

Built for your own material

Your sounds stay yours

Only sound names and lengths are sent to the arranger, never the audio. Processing runs on your computer.

Free effects included

Pitch, reverse, chop, stretch, filter, drive, delay and reverb come with the app at no extra cost.

Credits you can see

1 credit is 1 cent. Before a big run you see the most it can cost, and failed runs are free.

Dark studio look

Near-black panels, amber step lights and high contrast that stays easy on the eyes in long sessions.
