Questions

Good to know

What do credits pay for?

Only the AI that arranges your song from your prompt. Playing, editing, effects and exporting run on your computer and never use credits.

Can I use the starter sounds in songs I release?

Yes. The built-in drums, bass, keys and pads are made in code and come with no license strings.

What happens if a run fails?

It costs nothing. Before a big run you also see the most it can cost, and you can set your own cap per prompt in your account.

Do my recordings get uploaded?

No. Only sound names and lengths are sent to the arranger. Your audio stays on your computer.
