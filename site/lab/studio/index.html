Loopwright studio

Shortcuts
Real
  • Your files are decoded and played locally with the Web Audio API.
  • Built-in drums, bass, pad, keys and the example vocal are synthesized in code, so no licensed samples are involved.
  • 31 built-in pedals run live, each with knobs and presets: EQs, compressor, limiter, noise gate, ducker, overdrive, distortion, fuzz, saturator, bitcrusher, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, vibrato, auto-pan, auto-filter, echo, ping-pong, tape echo, room, hall, plate, widener and the sample pedals. Effects process in the order you chain them.
  • Panels pop out, float, resize and dock again, and the layout is remembered in this browser.
  • Meters, lights and the playhead follow the audio as it plays.
  • Record turns your keyboard playing into a pattern, and a mic take into a new sound and track, lined up with bar 1.
  • "Arrange" asks Claude for a pattern built only from your listed sounds when this view allows it. Otherwise a keyword matcher does it.
  • AI producer settings shape every run: personality, creativity, work mode, explained changes, takes, reference vibe, memory, Keep my sound, protected tracks, built-in sound rules, lyric helper, snapshots with undo, a monthly budget, and a view of what the AI is sent.
  • Play live turns any sound, yours included, into a playable instrument from the on-screen keys, your computer keyboard, or a MIDI keyboard where the browser allows it.
  • Export renders the song offline to a 16- or 24-bit WAV.
Faked or missing
  • No accounts, cloud library or saved projects yet. Refresh and the song is gone; your layout and settings stay.
  • The sample pedals (pitch, slicer, reverser, step gate, stretch) act on each hit before the chain, so their place on the board doesn't matter.
  • The ducker pumps on the beat. Ducking from another track's actual signal comes with the desktop app.
  • Reverbs use built-in synthetic rooms, not recordings of real spaces.
  • "Stretch" changes speed and pitch together. A real stretcher keeps the pitch.
  • No third-party plugins here. Browsers can't load VST3, AU or CLAP; the desktop app will, with a plugin manager that scans your plugin folders. Browser plugins (Web Audio Modules) are on the list for the web studio.
  • One 16-step pattern per track, played in the bars you switch on. Sections and longer clips come next.
  • No stem splitting, key detection or beat detection on your uploads.
  • Mic recording uses whatever mic the browser allows, with no monitoring or input choice.
